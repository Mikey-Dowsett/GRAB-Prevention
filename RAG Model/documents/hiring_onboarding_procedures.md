# Westbrook University — Staff Hiring and Onboarding Procedures
**Document ID:** HR-PROC-007  
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2023  
**Maintained by:** Office of Human Resources — Talent Acquisition

---

## Part I: Recruitment and Hiring

### 1.1 Position Approval

Before initiating any search for a new or replacement position, the hiring department must obtain a Position Authorization Form (PAF) signed by the department head and the relevant VP or Dean. For positions with a salary grade of 12 or above, additional review by the Provost or President's Office is required. Reactivating a frozen position requires re-authorization even if the position was previously approved.

### 1.2 Job Description Development

The hiring manager works with HR's Talent Acquisition team to finalize the job description. All job descriptions must include:
- Position title and FLSA classification (exempt/non-exempt)
- Salary grade and anticipated range
- Required and preferred qualifications
- Essential and marginal job functions
- Supervisory responsibilities (if any)
- Physical and environmental requirements

HR reviews all job descriptions for compliance with ADA, FLSA, and university classification standards prior to posting.

### 1.3 Posting

All staff positions must be posted publicly for a minimum of 10 business days before an offer can be extended. Positions are posted on the Westbrook University Careers page (careers.westbrook.edu) and at minimum one external job board. Internal-only postings may be approved for lateral transfers in limited circumstances with HR approval.

### 1.4 Applicant Screening

The hiring manager or a designated search committee reviews applications after the posting closes. HR provides an Applicant Screening Guide that outlines permissible and impermissible screening criteria. Hiring managers must document their screening rationale in PageUp (the university's applicant tracking system). At least three finalists must be identified for any phone or on-campus interview unless the applicant pool does not support this.

### 1.5 Interviews

Interview questions must be approved by HR before use. The following question types are prohibited:
- Questions about age, date of birth, or graduation year (unless directly tied to a bona fide occupational requirement)
- Questions about marital status, children, or family planning
- Questions about national origin, citizenship, or first language
- Questions about religious observance or practices
- Questions about disabilities or medical history

All members of a search committee must complete the online Equitable Hiring training module before participating in interviews. The training is available at training.westbrook.edu and must have been completed within the prior 24 months.

### 1.6 Reference Checks

HR conducts all formal reference checks using a standardized form. A minimum of two professional references must be verified before an offer is extended. The hiring manager may supplement with their own reference conversations, but these must also be documented in PageUp.

### 1.7 Extending an Offer

Only HR or an authorized HR representative may extend a formal offer of employment. Verbal offers may be made by the hiring manager but must be followed immediately by written confirmation from HR. All offers are contingent on successful completion of a background check (see HR-PROC-007B). Salary offers must fall within the approved grade range. Offers at or above the midpoint require VP or Dean approval.

## Part II: Pre-Employment Requirements

### 2.1 Background Check

All staff, and faculty with staff duties, must complete a background check through Sterling Verify before their start date. The standard check includes:
- Social Security number trace
- Criminal history (7-year county, statewide, and national criminal database)
- Sex offender registry
- Employment verification (last two employers)

Positions with financial authority, data access, or work with minors require an enhanced check that includes credit history and additional database searches. Results are reviewed by HR in compliance with the Fair Chance Hiring guidelines. A background check result does not automatically disqualify a candidate; HR conducts an individualized assessment.

### 2.2 I-9 Employment Eligibility Verification

All new hires must complete Section 1 of Form I-9 on or before their first day of work. Section 2 must be completed by HR within 3 business days of the start date. Original documents are required; remote employees may use an authorized remote I-9 verification service. The university participates in E-Verify.

### 2.3 Drug Screening

Drug screening is required for positions in Safety & Security, Facilities Management, Transportation, and Child and Family Programs. Testing is conducted through PrimaryCare Occupational Health. Offers are contingent on a negative result.

## Part III: Onboarding

### 3.1 New Hire Orientation

All new employees attend New Hire Orientation (NHO) on their first day. NHO is held every Monday at 9:00 AM in the Human Resources Conference Room (Benton Hall 102). For employees with a Tuesday–Friday start date, NHO is scheduled for the following Monday; HR will provide a self-guided onboarding checklist for the interim period.

NHO covers:
- University mission, values, and organizational structure
- Benefits enrollment (enrollment window begins on Day 1)
- Payroll and timekeeping systems
- Campus safety and emergency procedures
- IT account setup and acceptable use policy
- Key policies: Code of Conduct, Anti-Harassment, FERPA, and Remote Work

### 3.2 Department Onboarding

Hiring managers are responsible for completing the Department Onboarding Checklist (HR-FORM-007C) within the employee's first two weeks. This includes:
- Workspace and equipment setup
- Introduction to team members
- Review of role-specific duties and performance expectations
- Assignment of a peer buddy (recommended)
- First 30/60/90-day check-ins scheduled

### 3.3 Required Training

All new employees must complete the following training modules within 30 days of hire through the university's learning management system (Cornerstone):
- WB-101: Westbrook University Culture and Values (1 hr)
- HR-102: FERPA and Student Data Privacy (45 min)
- HR-103: Title IX and Anti-Harassment (1.5 hr)
- IT-104: Information Security Awareness (1 hr)
- EHS-105: Workplace Safety Fundamentals (30 min)

Failure to complete required training by the deadline will be noted in the employee's file and addressed in the 90-day check-in.

### 3.4 Probationary Period

All new staff employees serve a 6-month probationary period. During this period, employees may be separated from employment without following the standard progressive discipline process. A 90-day performance check-in and a 6-month end-of-probation review are conducted by the supervisor using forms HR-FORM-007D and HR-FORM-007E respectively.
