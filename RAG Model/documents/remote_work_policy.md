# Westbrook University — Remote and Hybrid Work Policy
**Policy Number:** HR-POL-022  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2023  
**Last Revised:** September 15, 2023  
**Applies to:** All Benefits-Eligible Staff (Non-Faculty)

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for remote and hybrid work arrangements at Westbrook University. It is intended to support employee flexibility while ensuring that university operations, student services, and team effectiveness are maintained at the highest level.

---

## 2. Definitions

**Remote Work:** An arrangement in which the employee performs all or a substantial portion of their duties from a location other than a university facility on a regular and recurring basis.

**Hybrid Work:** An arrangement in which the employee splits work time between a university facility and a remote location on a regular schedule.

**In-Person Work:** The traditional arrangement in which the employee performs all duties from a university facility.

**Primary Work Location:** The location from which the employee most regularly works, as designated in their work arrangement agreement.

---

## 3. Eligibility

Remote and hybrid work arrangements may be considered for positions where:
1. The core job duties can be effectively performed off-site
2. Performance metrics can be clearly measured and monitored
3. The arrangement does not negatively impact service delivery to students or other university stakeholders
4. Technology requirements can be met (see Section 7)

Positions requiring significant in-person interaction (front-desk reception, lab supervision, campus safety, facilities management, etc.) are generally not eligible for remote arrangements. Faculty work arrangements are governed separately by the Faculty Senate Agreement.

---

## 4. Types of Arrangements

### 4.1 Hybrid Schedule
The employee works on-campus a minimum of 2 days per week. The specific days must be designated in advance and approved by the supervisor. Ad hoc schedule changes require 48-hour notice where possible.

### 4.2 Primarily Remote
The employee works remotely 4–5 days per week and reports to campus as needed for meetings, events, or operational requirements. These arrangements require approval from both the department head and the Vice President of the relevant division, in addition to HR.

### 4.3 Fully Remote (Out-of-State)
The university may, in limited circumstances, approve fully remote work from a state other than Missouri. Such arrangements require additional review for payroll tax, workers' compensation, and employment law compliance. Approval is granted by the Provost or relevant VP and is documented on a separate Out-of-State Remote Work Agreement. The university does not currently approve fully remote work from outside the United States.

---

## 5. Application and Approval Process

1. Employee completes the Remote Work Arrangement Request Form (HR-FORM-022A) available on the HR portal
2. Employee's direct supervisor reviews and provides a written recommendation
3. Form is submitted to the department head for approval
4. HR reviews for policy compliance and classification concerns
5. Approved agreements are stored in the employee's personnel file and reviewed annually

Remote work arrangements are a privilege, not an entitlement. They may be modified or revoked at any time with two weeks' notice if operational needs change or if performance or conduct concerns arise.

---

## 6. Expectations for Remote Employees

Employees working remotely are expected to:
- Maintain regular working hours as agreed upon with their supervisor
- Be available by phone, email, and video conference during core hours (10:00 AM – 3:00 PM Central)
- Attend all required in-person meetings and university events
- Maintain a professional, private, and distraction-limited work environment
- Protect the confidentiality of university data and systems (see Section 7)
- Report any workplace injury that occurs during remote work hours to HR within 24 hours

---

## 7. Technology and Equipment

The university will provide standard equipment (laptop, headset) to approved remote employees. Employees are responsible for maintaining reliable high-speed internet access (minimum 25 Mbps download / 5 Mbps upload). The university does not reimburse personal internet or utility costs.

All remote work must be performed on university-issued or university-approved devices. Remote employees must:
- Use the university VPN when accessing internal systems
- Not use public or unsecured Wi-Fi without VPN active
- Keep devices updated with all required security patches within 72 hours of notification
- Comply with all policies in HR-POL-030 (Information Security Policy)

---

## 8. Workspace Safety

Remote employees are responsible for ensuring their home workspace meets basic ergonomic and safety standards. The university provides access to an online ergonomics assessment tool and a one-time $200 home office equipment stipend (taxable) for full-time employees transitioning to a hybrid or primarily remote arrangement. Receipts must be submitted to Accounts Payable within 90 days of arrangement start.

---

## 9. Performance Management

Remote work arrangements do not alter performance standards or expectations. Supervisors will set clear, measurable goals at the beginning of each review period. If performance concerns arise, the supervisor may require increased on-site attendance as part of a performance improvement plan, or may revoke the remote arrangement pending resolution.

---

## 10. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually by the Office of Human Resources and the Remote Work Advisory Committee. Employee feedback may be submitted through the HR portal under "Policy Feedback."
