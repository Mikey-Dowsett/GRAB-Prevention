# Westbrook University — Benefits Enrollment Guide
**Plan Year:** January 1, 2024 – December 31, 2024  
**Document ID:** HR-BEN-003  
**Open Enrollment Period:** October 15 – November 15, 2023

---

## Overview

This guide provides a summary of benefits available to eligible Westbrook University employees. Enrollment is conducted online through the BenefitConnect portal at benefits.westbrook.edu. Changes to benefits outside the open enrollment window require a qualifying life event (QLE). Contact HR at (816) 555-0101 for questions.

---

## Eligibility

Full-time employees (0.75 FTE and above) are eligible for benefits on the first day of the month following 30 days of employment. Part-time employees working an average of 30+ hours per week as determined under the ACA measurement period may also be eligible. Dependents include legal spouses, domestic partners (with documentation), and dependent children up to age 26.

---

## Medical Insurance

Westbrook University offers three medical plan options through BlueCrest Health:

### Plan A — High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with HSA
- **Monthly Premium (Employee only):** $42.00
- **Monthly Premium (Employee + Spouse):** $198.00
- **Monthly Premium (Family):** $284.00
- **Annual Deductible:** $1,500 individual / $3,000 family
- **Out-of-Pocket Maximum:** $4,000 individual / $8,000 family
- **University HSA Contribution:** $750/year (individual), $1,500/year (family)
- **Coinsurance after deductible:** 80/20

### Plan B — Preferred Provider Organization (PPO)
- **Monthly Premium (Employee only):** $112.00
- **Monthly Premium (Employee + Spouse):** $356.00
- **Monthly Premium (Family):** $498.00
- **Annual Deductible:** $500 individual / $1,000 family
- **Copay (Primary Care):** $25
- **Copay (Specialist):** $50
- **Copay (Urgent Care):** $75
- **Emergency Room:** $250 (waived if admitted)

### Plan C — Health Maintenance Organization (HMO)
- **Monthly Premium (Employee only):** $78.00
- **Monthly Premium (Employee + Spouse):** $262.00
- **Monthly Premium (Family):** $382.00
- **Annual Deductible:** $0
- **Copay (Primary Care):** $15
- **Copay (Specialist):** $40
- Requires PCP referrals for specialist visits; network-only coverage

## Dental Insurance

Dental coverage is provided through DentalPlus Network.

- **Monthly Premium (Employee only):** $8.40
- **Monthly Premium (Employee + Family):** $22.60
- **Annual Maximum Benefit:** $2,000 per person
- **Preventive Care:** 100% covered (in-network)
- **Basic Services (fillings, extractions):** 80% covered after $50 deductible
- **Major Services (crowns, bridges):** 50% covered after $50 deductible
- **Orthodontia (children under 19):** 50% up to $1,500 lifetime max

---

## Vision Insurance

Vision coverage is provided through ClearVision EyeCare.

- **Monthly Premium (Employee only):** $5.10
- **Monthly Premium (Employee + Family):** $12.80
- **Eye Exam:** $10 copay, once per calendar year
- **Frames or Contact Lenses:** $150 allowance per year

---

## Life Insurance and AD&D

The university provides basic life insurance at no cost to the employee equal to 1x annual salary (up to $300,000). Employees may purchase supplemental life insurance up to 4x annual salary, subject to evidence of insurability above $200,000. Accidental Death and Dismemberment (AD&D) coverage is included at the same benefit level as life insurance.

---

## Disability Insurance

### Short-Term Disability (STD)
- Provided at no cost to employee
- Covers 60% of base salary after a 7-day elimination period
- Maximum benefit duration: 12 weeks

### Long-Term Disability (LTD)
- Provided at no cost to employee
- Covers 60% of base salary after 90-day elimination period
- Coverage continues until age 65 or return to work (whichever comes first)

---

## Retirement Plans

### 403(b) Defined Contribution Plan
Westbrook University offers a 403(b) retirement plan through Vanguard. Employees may contribute pre-tax or Roth (after-tax) dollars.

- **University Match:** 100% of first 3% of salary contributed, then 50% of next 2% (maximum match = 4% of salary)
- **Vesting Schedule:** 100% immediate vesting on employee contributions; university match vests 25% per year over 4 years
- **2024 IRS Contribution Limit:** $23,000 ($30,500 for employees age 50+)

### Roth IRA Option
Employees may also contribute to a Roth IRA through payroll deduction via Vanguard.

---

## Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA)

### Healthcare FSA
- Annual Election Max: $3,050
- Funds are available on January 1; use-it-or-lose-it applies (up to $610 rollover permitted)

### Dependent Care FSA
- Annual Election Max: $5,000 per household
- For qualifying dependent care expenses (childcare, elder care)

## Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

The university provides access to LifeBalance EAP at no cost to all employees and their household members. Services include:
- 6 free counseling sessions per issue per year
- Financial counseling and legal consultation
- Work-life referrals (childcare, eldercare, pet care, etc.)
- 24/7 crisis line: 1-888-555-0300

---

## Tuition Benefits

### Employee Tuition Waiver
Full-time employees may take up to 6 credit hours per semester at Westbrook University tuition-free (subject to seat availability after the second week of class). Courses must be taken outside of scheduled work hours unless approved by supervisor.

### Dependent Tuition Exchange
Eligible dependents may apply for tuition exchange at participating institutions through the Tuition Exchange Consortium. Applications are due February 1 each year. Award is not guaranteed and is subject to program caps.
