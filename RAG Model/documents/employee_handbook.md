# Westbrook University — Employee Handbook
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Document ID:** HR-POL-001  
**Maintained by:** Office of Human Resources

---

## 1. Welcome Statement

Welcome to Westbrook University. We are committed to fostering an inclusive, equitable, and dynamic workplace for all faculty, staff, and administrators. This handbook outlines your rights, responsibilities, and the policies that govern employment at the university. All employees are expected to review this handbook in full and acknowledge receipt via the HR portal.

---

## 2. Employment Classifications

### 2.1 Full-Time Faculty
Faculty holding a 1.0 FTE appointment with a tenure-track, tenured, or non-tenure-track designation. Full-time faculty are eligible for all university benefits.

### 2.2 Part-Time / Adjunct Faculty
Faculty appointed at less than 1.0 FTE on a semester-by-semester basis. Benefits eligibility is governed by the ACA and university policy HR-POL-014.

### 2.3 Staff (Exempt)
Staff employees classified as exempt under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA). Exempt employees are not eligible for overtime compensation.

### 2.4 Staff (Non-Exempt)
Staff employees classified as non-exempt under FLSA. Non-exempt employees must record hours worked and are eligible for overtime at 1.5x their base rate for hours exceeding 40 per workweek.

### 2.5 Temporary and Casual Employees
Employees hired on a short-term basis for a period not to exceed 6 months in a 12-month period. Not eligible for benefits.

---

## 3. Work Hours and Schedules

Standard university business hours are 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Individual department schedules may vary. Flexible work arrangements may be requested through a supervisor and require approval from the department head and Human Resources. Remote work arrangements are governed by Policy HR-POL-022.

---

## 4. Time Off and Leave Policies

### 4.1 Vacation
Full-time staff accrue vacation leave at the following rates:
- Years 0–2: 10 days per year
- Years 3–7: 15 days per year
- Years 8+: 20 days per year

Faculty vacation is governed by the Faculty Senate Agreement, Section 7.

### 4.2 Sick Leave
All full-time employees accrue sick leave at a rate of 1 day per month (12 days per year). Unused sick leave may be carried over up to a maximum of 120 days.

### 4.3 University Holidays
Westbrook University observes the following paid holidays:
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day and the day after
- Winter Break (December 24 – January 1)

### 4.4 Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)
Employees who have worked at least 12 months and at least 1,250 hours in the prior year are eligible for up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave under the FMLA. Requests must be submitted to HR no fewer than 30 days in advance when foreseeable.

---

## 5. Code of Conduct

All Westbrook University employees are expected to maintain the highest standards of professional conduct. This includes:
- Treating colleagues, students, and visitors with respect and dignity
- Maintaining confidentiality of student records under FERPA
- Avoiding conflicts of interest
- Reporting suspected misconduct through the university's Ethics Hotline (1-800-555-0182)

Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

---

## 6. Anti-Discrimination and Equal Opportunity Policy

Westbrook University is an equal opportunity employer. The university does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, national origin, sex, age, disability, veteran status, sexual orientation, gender identity, or any other status protected by law. Employees who believe they have experienced discrimination should contact the Office of Equity and Inclusion at equity@westbrook.edu or call (816) 555-0190.

---

## 7. Performance Reviews

All staff employees receive an annual performance review conducted by their direct supervisor. Reviews take place each year in October. Faculty performance is evaluated through the annual review process governed by the Faculty Senate Agreement. Performance ratings are: Outstanding, Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Needs Improvement, and Unsatisfactory.

---

## 8. Separation from Employment

### 8.1 Resignation
Employees are expected to provide a minimum of two weeks' written notice. Department heads and directors should provide at least four weeks' notice.

### 8.2 Retirement
Employees planning to retire should notify HR and their supervisor at least 90 days in advance to ensure proper transition planning.

### 8.3 Involuntary Separation
The university reserves the right to terminate employment at any time. Employees may be released for cause following a documented progressive discipline process, or as part of a reduction in force (RIF). Severance eligibility is determined on a case-by-case basis.
