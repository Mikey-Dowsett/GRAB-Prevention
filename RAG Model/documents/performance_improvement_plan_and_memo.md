# MEMO

**TO:** Department Heads and Supervisors  
**FROM:** Office of Human Resources  
**DATE:** October 3, 2023  
**RE:** Annual Performance Review Cycle — FY2024 Guidelines and Deadlines

---

As we approach the FY2024 annual review cycle, HR would like to remind all supervisors of key dates, updated procedures, and training resources.

**Review Window:** October 1 – November 30, 2023  
**Final Completed Reviews Due to HR:** December 8, 2023  
**Merit Increase Letters Distributed:** January 5, 2024 (pending budget approval)

### What's New This Year

This year we are piloting an updated competency framework for all non-exempt and exempt staff. The new framework introduces five core competencies that apply university-wide:

1. **Accountability** — Takes ownership of work and outcomes
2. **Collaboration** — Works effectively with colleagues across functions
3. **Communication** — Conveys information clearly and listens actively
4. **Adaptability** — Navigates change with professionalism and openness
5. **Student/Mission Focus** — Aligns work to the university's educational mission

Each competency is rated on a 1–5 scale in the new review form, available in Cornerstone HR as of October 1.

### Process Reminders

- Employees complete a self-evaluation first; supervisors then complete their ratings independently. Do not share your ratings with employees before meeting with them.
- The review conversation should be forward-looking. Discuss development goals for FY2024, not just a recap of the past year.
- All reviews must be reviewed by the department head before being finalized. Department heads do not need to sign off on each individual review, but must attest that all reviews in their area were completed and submitted by the deadline.
- Reviews with a rating of "Needs Improvement" or "Unsatisfactory" must be reviewed with HR before being shared with the employee. Contact hr@westbrook.edu to schedule a consultation.

### Merit Increase Budget

The university has approved a merit pool of 3.0% for eligible staff. Employees on an active PIP or with a final rating of "Unsatisfactory" are not eligible for a merit increase. Distribution of the pool is at supervisor discretion within ranges approved by HR and the relevant VP.
